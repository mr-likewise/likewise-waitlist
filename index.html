FAQs

How do more answers lead to more insight?

Each answer adds another point of context to your Decision Profile. Over time, you can look across your responses to notice recurring choices and changes in your outlook.

Where does the data come from?

Where reliable published Canadian research exists, including national surveys, Likewise shows it alongside your answer and names the source. The charts on this page are sample previews.

How does Likewise work?

Answer a question about everyday life, then see how your response compares with other Canadians’ answers.

How do I join the waitlist?

Enter your email in the waitlist field and select Send. You’ll finish signing up on the Likewise signup form.

How is my information used?

We use your email for waitlist updates. We don’t sell your information, and you can ask us to delete your waitlist details at any time.

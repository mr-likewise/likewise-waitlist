Why Likewise

Comparisons with context.

An answer means more when you understand the people and experiences behind it.

  • Published research

    Canadian research, with the source named

    Where reliable published research exists, such as national surveys, we show it alongside your answer and tell you where it comes from. It's a useful reference point for how Canadians as a whole respond.

  • Likewise members

    Comparisons that grow with participation

    As more people take part, you'll also be able to compare with other Likewise members. Member results reflect the people who answered, not all Canadians, and narrower groups open up only when enough people have answered.

Your data should work for you.

Companies learn from the information you share. Likewise is built to give you something back: comparisons with context, insights from your answers, and opportunities to take part in rewards.

Participation may offer opportunities to enter sponsor-funded reward draws.

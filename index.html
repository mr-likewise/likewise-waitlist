Why Likewise

Your data should
work for you.

Every company you interact with uses your financial behaviour to make decisions. Likewise gives you something back.

Honest peer benchmarks
Not national averages. Not editorial estimates. Real answers from Canadians matched to your actual demographic situation, by age, city, income range, and household type. The more people who join, the sharper that comparison gets.
Your financial identity, owned by you
Over time, Likewise builds you a Financial Health Profile, a real record of your financial behaviour that you own, control, and can share with lenders on your own terms. Richer than a credit score. Yours to keep or delete.
Independent. No bank behind this.
Likewise is not owned by a bank, a broker, or a financial product company. We don't recommend products unless you ask for help, and when we do, we tell you it's sponsored. No hidden agenda. No algorithmic nudges.
